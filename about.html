About us

A chess academy built around clear steps

Matrix Chess Academy is a chess school in Basaveshwara Nagar, Shivamogga. We help children and adults learn the game properly and enjoy it for life.

What we believe

Chess teaches children to think before they move

Chess builds focus, patience and the habit of looking one move ahead. We want every child to feel those benefits, not only the ones who become strong players.

That's why our training is split into clear levels. A student always knows what they are working on now and what comes next, and parents can see the progress for themselves.

Our students learn from structured lessons, our own graded puzzle books, real games against each other, and a review of their games so mistakes turn into lessons.

Head coach

Sunil

Sunil leads the coaching at Matrix Chess Academy. He writes the academy's training material, including the Matrix Tactics puzzle books, and builds the tools the academy uses to track every student's progress.

His classes focus on clear explanations, plenty of practice, and helping each student enjoy the game while they improve.

How we teach

Lessons, puzzles, games, review

One idea per class

A short, focused lesson on a single theme, explained with board examples children can follow.

Puzzles from our own books

Students solve puzzles on that theme from Matrix Tactics, graded by level and rating.

Practice games

Students play each other in class and online, then we go over the key moments together.

Progress you can see

We track online ratings and topics covered, and share regular updates with parents.
