Programs

Chess classes in Shivamogga

Four levels take a student from the first moves to confident tournament play. Every student starts with a short assessment, and each level ends with a test and a certificate.

3 months

Beginner

For complete beginners. Children learn how every piece moves and start playing full games with confidence.

Who it's for: Children who are new to chess.

What students learn

  • How every piece moves and captures
  • Check, checkmate and stalemate
  • Castling, en passant and pawn promotion
  • Basic checkmates with queen and rook
  • Simple opening rules
3 months

Intermediate

For students who know the rules. The focus moves to tactics, the patterns that win material and games.

Who it's for: Students who know the rules and play full games.

What students learn

  • Forks, pins, skewers and double attacks
  • Discovered attacks and removing the defender
  • Opening principles in real games
  • Simple king and pawn endgames
3 months

Tournament Prep

For players getting ready for their first rated tournaments, on and off the board.

Who it's for: Players preparing for district and state events.

What students learn

  • Using a chess clock and writing the score sheet
  • Tournament rules and etiquette
  • Building a simple opening repertoire
  • Reviewing games after every round
9 months

Advanced

For tournament players who want to raise their rating with deeper, personal training.

Who it's for: Rated or regular tournament players.

What students learn

  • Strategy and positional play
  • Deeper endgame technique
  • Calculation training
  • A personal plan built from the student's own games
Inside a class

How every class runs

The same rhythm in every level, so children settle in quickly and parents always know what their child is working on.

One idea per class

A short, focused lesson on a single theme, explained with board examples children can follow.

Puzzles from our own books

Students solve puzzles on that theme from Matrix Tactics, graded by level and rating.

Practice games

Students play each other in class and online, then we go over the key moments together.

Progress you can see

We track online ratings and topics covered, and share regular updates with parents.

Questions

Questions parents ask

Does my child need to know chess before joining?

No. The Beginner level starts from how the pieces move. Children who already play join a higher level after a short assessment.

What age can my child start?

Most children are ready once they can sit through a short lesson and recognise the pieces. Bring your child for a demo class and we'll suggest the right level.

How long is each level?

Beginner, Intermediate and Tournament Prep are three months each. The Advanced level runs for nine months.

How do you track my child's progress?

We track topics covered and online ratings, and each level ends with a test and a certificate before the student moves up.

Do students get study material?

Yes. Students practise from Matrix Tactics, our own graded puzzle book, along with puzzles and games set in class.
